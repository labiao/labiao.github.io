Daoyuan Zheng

Remote Sensing · Computer Vision · Weakly Supervised Learning

I am a researcher at Wuhan University. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and remote sensing, with a focus on learning from limited supervision and extracting reliable geographic information from high-resolution imagery.

My current interests include weakly and semi-supervised semantic segmentation, change detection, multimodal remote sensing, and intelligent interpretation of cultural-heritage landscapes.

I enjoy turning research ideas into reproducible open-source projects. You can find code, models, and datasets for recent work on my GitHub profile.
